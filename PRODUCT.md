# Kumo — Product

Platform: android (native, Kotlin + Jetpack Compose). Mode: Operate (daily tool), with Experience moments in Focus/Companion flows.

**Who:** one person — a B.Tech CSE student (college 08:00–14:00, evening gym, sleep 22:30–06:30) turning 2027 into a year of becoming an Applied AI/ML Engineer. Uses the app many short times a day: morning overview, between classes, at the gym, at night review.

**Name:** Kumo (renamed from ASCEND 2027 on 2026-11-26, user's choice). Points currency is called honey.

**Tagline:** Build the person you want to become.

**Job:** know what to do next, log it in seconds, see the year compound. Offline-first; nothing is invented (nutrition, AI replies, stats come only from the user's own entries).

**Tone:** calm, warm, quietly ambitious. A companion, not a drill sergeant. Rewards never gate core features.

**Anti-references:** the Stitch "Obsidian Kinetic" export (mint-on-charcoal, robot "Nova", hype copy like "Daily Velocity Matrix", "prefrontal cortex is peaked"), generic fitness-app neon, Duolingo's owl or any existing app's character.
